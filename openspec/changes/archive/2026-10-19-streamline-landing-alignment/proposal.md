## Why

The landing page uses different column positions and vertical alignment for related sections, while the same call workflow appears in a capability strip, four-step list, calling checklist, and timeline. The user requested a Taste-guided alignment and redundancy cleanup.

## What Changes

- Align hero and role content on one shared grid and center header navigation independently of brand width.
- Keep one ordered workflow; consolidate telephony context into its integration note.
- Remove repeated capability strip, calling explanation/timeline, closing sign-in block, and role marketing subheadings.
- Keep two sign-in entry points, in the header and hero, and update navigation to the remaining sections.
- Preserve branding, themes, accessibility, and the previous removal of the Agent Screen illustration.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `public-landing-experience`: Specify aligned, concise presentation with one workflow explanation and consistent navigation.

## Impact

Welcome Blade, scoped landing CSS, landing feature tests, and public landing specification. No backend, database, dependencies, or authentication behavior changes.
